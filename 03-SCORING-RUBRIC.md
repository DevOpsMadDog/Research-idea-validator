# Scoring Rubric (For Final Judge)

**Model:** GPT-5.2

---

Score each dimension 0–10 and justify:

## Dimensions

1. **Insurability** (0-10)
   - Can risks be insured under conservative actuarial principles?
   - Are parametric triggers objectively verifiable?
   - Is correlation risk controlled?
   - Is moral hazard manageable?

2. **Regulatory Feasibility** (0-10)
   - Can this launch in India without regulatory blockers?
   - Is the legal structure safe?
   - Are compliance requirements manageable?
   - Is mis-selling risk controlled?

3. **Distribution Strength** (0-10)
   - Can creators be acquired cost-effectively?
   - Is the product compelling enough for adoption?
   - Are there natural distribution channels?
   - Is pricing acceptable to creators?

4. **Moat/Defensibility** (0-10)
   - Is there a data advantage?
   - Are there network effects?
   - Can competitors replicate easily?
   - Is platform dependency manageable?

5. **Unit Economics Realism** (0-10)
   - Is CAC < LTV?
   - Is gross margin sustainable?
   - Can the business reach profitability?
   - Is the path to scale clear?

6. **Fraud Resistance** (0-10)
   - Can parametric triggers be gamed?
   - Are fraud controls effective?
   - Is adverse selection manageable?
   - Can collusion be detected?

7. **Platform Dependency Risk** (0-10)
   - What happens if APIs are revoked?
   - Can platforms compete directly?
   - Is there a fallback strategy?
   - Is multi-platform support viable?

8. **Investor Attractiveness** (0-10)
   - Overall score considering all factors
   - Would conservative VCs invest?
   - Is the risk/reward profile acceptable?
   - Is the team/execution risk manageable?

---

**Instructions:** Use this rubric when synthesizing the final judge output. Provide 1-2 sentence justification for each score.
