## ✅ INVESTMENT DECISION CRITERIA

### Green Flags (What Makes This Fundable)

- ✅ Large, underserved market (150K+ creators in India)
- ✅ No direct competitors (first-mover in creator risk + insurance)
- ✅ Strong unit economics (LTV:CAC = 6.9:1)
- ✅ Multiple revenue streams (SaaS, insurance, B2B, lending)
- ✅ Path to profitability (Year 3 EBITDA positive)
- ✅ Proven regulatory path (MGA model; Acko/Digit precedent)
- ✅ Conservative assumptions (risk-averse by design)

### Red Flags (What Could Kill This)

- 🚩 Platform dependency (YouTube controls our destiny)
- 🚩 No insurer partnership yet (70% will reject)
- 🚩 Weak initial moat (TubeBuddy/VidIQ can clone in 3 months)
- 🚩 Long time to PMF (18-24 months)
- 🚩 Fraud risk (India has high payment fraud)
- 🚩 Regulatory unpredictability (IRDAI can change rules)
- 🚩 Founder execution risk (3-domain problem: insurance + creator + fintech)

### Investment Recommendation

**INVEST IF:**
- ✅ Team has insurance expertise (ex-Acko, ex-actuary, or strong advisor)
- ✅ Team has creator distribution (founder is creator OR deep connections)
- ✅ Early traction exists (100+ users in beta, strong engagement)
- ✅ Founders commit to 5-year build (patient capital)

**PASS IF:**
- ❌ Founders lack insurance expertise (too risky without)
- ❌ Trying to launch insurance without licensed partner (illegal)
- ❌ Trying to cover "revenue drops" (uninsurable)
- ❌ No plan for platform diversification (YouTube-only = too risky)
