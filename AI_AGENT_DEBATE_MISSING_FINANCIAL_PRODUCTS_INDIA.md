## 🔔 ROUND 1: "What is the MOST CRITICAL missing financial product in India?"

### Agent DISRUPTOR opens:
> "The answer is blindingly obvious: **Income Share Agreements (ISAs)**. 
>

> - Average education loan default rate: 9.2%
> - Only 23% of graduates are employable
>

>

### Agent BANKER counters:
> "Classic techbro thinking - solve a complex socioeconomic problem with a financial instrument.
>
> Let me tell you why ISAs will FAIL catastrophically in India:
>
> 1. **Income verification is a nightmare**: 60% of India works in the informal economy. How do you collect 15% of income from a chai wallah's cash earnings?
>
> 2. **Legal enforceability**: Indian contract law and debt recovery (SARFAESI, DRT) took DECADES to mature for traditional loans. ISAs have ZERO legal precedent.
>
> 3. **Adverse selection death spiral**: Only students who expect LOW earnings will choose ISAs. The good candidates will take traditional loans. This destroys the actuarial model.
>
> The REAL missing product is **proper pension coverage**. 93% of India's workforce has NO retirement savings. That's 450 million people heading toward old-age poverty. But I suppose that's not 'sexy' enough for Shark Tank!"

### Agent REGULATOR intervenes:
> "Both of you are missing the regulatory elephant in the room.
>
> ISAs are legally ambiguous - are they loans? Securities? Employment contracts? Until Parliament clarifies, SEBI and RBI will play jurisdictional hot potato.
>

>
> I argue the critical gap is **parametric insurance** for climate resilience:
> - 120 million farmers with NO crop insurance
> - PM Fasal Bima Yojana covers only 30% and has 40% claim rejection
> - Parametric insurance (automatic payout on rainfall index) eliminates fraud AND paperwork
>
> This isn't innovation for innovation's sake - it's survival infrastructure for 600 million rural Indians!"

### Agent ECONOMIST synthesizes:
> "Fascinating how we're debating products when the real gap is **financial identity infrastructure**.
>
