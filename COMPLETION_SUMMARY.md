## 🎓 Key Learnings from Debate

### 1. India's Opportunity
- 800M Indians underserved in financial services
- ₹13+ trillion in market opportunities
- Infrastructure ready (UPI, Account Aggregator)

### 2. What Doesn't Work
- ❌ Build first, regulate later (Paytm lesson)
- ❌ Complex products in funding winter
- ❌ Vision without unit economics
- ❌ Underestimating technical costs

### 3. What Works
- ✅ Build on existing rails (Account Aggregator)
- ✅ Start with savings before credit
- ✅ Regulatory compliance from Day 1
- ✅ Focus on engagement over acquisition

### 4. Success Formula
**Vision** (serve underserved) + **Viability** (proven tech) + **Velocity** (ship fast) = **SAMPOORNA**
