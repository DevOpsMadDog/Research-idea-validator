- **Recurring revenue**: Monthly subscriptions = predictable income

- **Competition exists**: Calendly, Cal.com, others in market

## 9. RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Can't Close Enough Clients
**Mitigation:**
- Lower prices if needed ($799 instead of $1299)
- Offer "results guarantee" (refund if not page 1 in 90 days)
- Target easier-to-rank niches (less competitive cities)
- Hire experienced sales closer on commission (20-30%)

### Risk 2: Can't Deliver Results
**Mitigation:**
- Only promise what's achievable (page 1 for long-tail keywords)
- Set realistic expectations (90-120 days for results)
- Show progress reports monthly
- Hire experienced SEO freelancers if needed

### Risk 3: Cash Flow Issues
**Mitigation:**
- Charge 100% upfront for setup (most will pay)
- Offer small discount for annual prepay
- Use client payments to fund operations (no external funding needed)

### Risk 4: Team Quality Issues
**Mitigation:**
- Create detailed SOPs and training materials
- Hire from reputable platforms (Upwork, Freelancer)
- Quality check all work before client delivery
- Replace underperformers quickly
